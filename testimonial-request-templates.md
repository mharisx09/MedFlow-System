## Tips

- Ask 3–5 clients; expect 2–3 to reply. Follow up once after 3–4 days.
- Ask about a *specific result* if you know one (e.g. "the 40% drop in
  denials you mentioned in June") — it makes the quote concrete.
- Keep permission explicit: "Can I publish this with your name?" before it
  goes on the site.
